How to book a hotel in Tartus from abroad

For Syrians abroad and their families: how to book a seafront room in Tartus without international booking sites.

Why you will not find us on booking sites

International booking sites do not list hotels in Syria yet, so the way to book is directly with the hotel. That also means no commission and the hotel's own rates. See the room rates.

Step 1: send a request

Choose your dates and room in the booking form on this website, or write on WhatsApp at +963 998 730 730. You see the total and whether rooms are free straight away, and you get a booking number. Send a booking request.

Step 2: the deposit inside Syria

The hotel replies on WhatsApp with the deposit details. The deposit is paid by money transfer inside Syria, so a relative or friend in Syria can send it for you. As soon as it arrives, the hotel confirms the booking and sends you a confirmation letter.

Step 3: arrival

Bring the confirmation or your booking number. Check-in is from 14:00, reception is open 24 hours and parking is free for guests. The balance is paid at the hotel in cash, in US dollars.

Families and summer visits

Summer is the busiest season on the coast, so book early. Triple rooms take up to three guests, and the Royal Suite with its connecting room takes up to four. Rooms for families.

Quick answers

Can I book a hotel in Tartus on Booking.com?

International booking sites do not list hotels in Syria yet. Book directly on our website or on WhatsApp.

Can someone in Syria pay the deposit for me?

Yes. The deposit is paid by money transfer inside Syria, so a relative or friend there can send it on your behalf.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

Once the deposit arrives, the hotel confirms on WhatsApp and sends a confirmation letter with your booking number.

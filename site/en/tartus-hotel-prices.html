Hotel prices in Tartus

What a night costs at a 4-star hotel on the Tartus Corniche, with tax included and nothing added at the desk.

Room rates per night

Room typeGuestsPrice per night (tax included)
Standard Room1–2 guests$90 / $100
Triple RoomUp to 3 guests$130
Junior SuiteUp to 2 guestsRate on request
Royal Suite with connecting roomUp to 4 guestsRate on request

Breakfast is optional, $10 per person per day. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival.

A Standard Room is $90 for one guest and $100 for two. A Triple Room with three single beds is $130. These rates include tax. Suite rates are given on request. See every room and suite.

What the rate includes

Every room and suite has a balcony facing the sea. Electricity runs 24 hours, reception is open around the clock and parking is free for guests. Breakfast is optional at $10 per person.

How to book a hotel in Tartus

International booking sites do not list hotels in Syria yet, so the simplest way is to book with the hotel itself. Choose your dates on our booking form to see the total and whether rooms are free, or write on WhatsApp at +963 998 730 730. No card is needed: a deposit by money transfer inside Syria confirms the booking.

Payment and cancellation

The booking is confirmed once a deposit arrives by money transfer inside Syria; the hotel sends you the details on WhatsApp. The balance is paid at the hotel in cash, in US dollars. Check your dates.

Where the hotel is

Royal Inn stands on the Sea Corniche, with the old town and the boats to Arwad Island a short ride away. About staying on the seafront.

Quick answers

How much is a hotel room in Tartus?

At Royal Inn a Standard Room is $90 for one guest and $100 for two per night, and a Triple Room is $130, tax included.

Can I book a Tartus hotel on Booking.com?

International booking sites do not list hotels in Syria yet. Book directly on our website or by WhatsApp.

Do I need a credit card to book?

No. A deposit by money transfer inside Syria confirms the booking, and the balance is paid at the hotel in cash.

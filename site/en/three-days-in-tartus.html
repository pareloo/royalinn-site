Three days in Tartus: a simple itinerary

The sea, an island, a Phoenician city and two Crusader castles: how to see the highlights of Tartus in three days, starting from the Corniche.

Day 1: the Corniche, the museum and the old town

Start slowly with breakfast and a walk along the Sea Corniche. Late morning, visit the Cathedral of Our Lady of Tortosa, a 12th- and 13th-century building that has housed the Tartus Museum since 1956, about 0.8 km from the hotel in a straight line.

Walk on through the lanes of the old town, built inside the walls of the Crusader citadel, and be back on the Corniche for sunset: the sun sets straight into the sea.

Day 2: Arwad Island and Amrit

Take a morning boat from the harbour to Arwad, about 3 km offshore; the crossing takes about 20 minutes. Allow two to three hours for the lanes, the 13th-century citadel and the boatyards. Arwad Island guide.

In the afternoon drive about 6 km south to Amrit, the Phoenician city with its rock-cut temple and tower tombs. Amrit guide.

Day 3: Safita and Krak des Chevaliers

Leave early for the hills. Safita Tower is about 23 km from the hotel and Krak des Chevaliers, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, about 40 km, in a straight line; the roads are longer. Back by evening for a last night by the sea. Day trip guide.

Good to know

Boat times, opening days and road conditions change: ask reception the evening before. Carry cash. In summer, do the outdoor sights in the morning and keep the afternoon for the sea.

Stay on the Corniche: every room at Royal Inn has a sea-view balcony, from $90 a night with tax. Book your stay.

Quick answers

How many days do you need in Tartus?

Three days is enough for the Corniche, the museum and old town, Arwad, Amrit and a day trip to Safita and Krak des Chevaliers.

How far is Krak des Chevaliers from Tartus?

About 40 km in a straight line from the Corniche; the road is longer.

Privacy Policy

What this website does with your data, and what it does not.

What we do not collect

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or chat widgets, and it sets no cookies for visitors.

Language choice

Your language choice is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Booking requests

When you send the booking form, your name, phone number, optional email, dates, guests, room type and note are stored on the hotel's own server so that the hotel can confirm your stay. We do not ask for passport or card details.

Requests are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

If you choose WhatsApp instead, your message goes through WhatsApp, a service operated by Meta with its own privacy policy.

We use what you send us only to answer your request and arrange your stay, and we never sell or share it.

Server logs

Like most websites, the server keeps technical logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security. They are deleted after a short period.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to res@royalinn.org.

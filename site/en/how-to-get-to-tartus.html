How to get to Tartus

The main road routes to the Syrian coast from Lebanon, Damascus, Latakia and Turkey, and what to expect when you reach the Corniche.

Before you set off

Tartus is on the Mediterranean coast of Syria, between Latakia to the north and the Lebanese border to the south. Most guests arrive by road. Border crossings, flight schedules, entry and visa rules, travel times and fares all change, sometimes at short notice, so check current official information before you travel. The hotel is happy to tell you what recent guests have found: write on WhatsApp or call +963 998 730 730.

From Lebanon: Beirut and Tripoli

From Beirut the road runs north along the Lebanese coast to Tripoli and on towards the border. The Arida crossing, near the sea in the far north of Lebanon, is the coastal route into Syria and leads straight down the coast road to Tartus. Other crossings lead inland towards Homs, from where a highway runs west to the coast.

Crossings between Lebanon and Syria have closed and reopened several times, so confirm which ones are open, and their hours, on the day you plan to cross.

From Damascus via Homs

From Damascus, take the main highway north to Homs, then the road west through the gap in the mountains to the coast at Tartus. This is the usual route for guests who fly into Damascus International Airport. Shared taxis, buses and private drivers all use it; ask the hotel if you would like help finding a driver.

From Latakia along the coast

Latakia is north of Tartus on the same coast, and the coastal highway links the two cities, with the sea on one side and the hills on the other. Latakia airport has been under rehabilitation, so check whether commercial flights are operating before you plan around it.

From Turkey

From Turkey, travellers enter Syria by one of the land crossings on the northern border and continue south by road, either towards Latakia and then down the coast, or inland via Aleppo and Homs and then west to Tartus. Which crossings are open to which travellers, and what documents they ask for, changes, so check with official sources before you leave. There are also flights between Istanbul and Damascus. Arriving on the Corniche

Royal Inn is on the Sea Corniche, the seafront road of Tartus, facing the Mediterranean. Parking is free for guests, and reception is open at all hours, so a late arrival is no problem; just tell us roughly when you expect to arrive. See the hotel's location on the map.

Quick answers

How do I get to Tartus from Beirut?

By road north through Tripoli to the Lebanese–Syrian border, usually by the Arida crossing on the coast, then south along the coast road to Tartus. Check which crossings are open before you travel.

How do I get to Tartus from Damascus?

Take the highway north to Homs, then the road west to the coast at Tartus. Buses, shared taxis and private drivers use this route.

Is there an airport in Tartus?

No. The nearest airports are Damascus and Latakia. Latakia airport has been under rehabilitation, so check current flights before you book.

Does the hotel have parking?

Yes. Parking at Royal Inn is free for guests.

Can the hotel help me plan the journey?

Yes. Send your route and dates on WhatsApp to +963 998 730 730 and the hotel will tell you what it knows about the current situation.

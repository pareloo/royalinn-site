Blog & Tartus guide

A family hotel in Tartus

Rooms for three, a suite for four, free parking and the Corniche in front of the hotel.

Rooms for families

The Triple Room has three single beds and a sea-view balcony, at $130 a night with tax included. The Royal Suite is booked together with its own connecting room, for up to four guests; the connecting room is charged at the Standard Room rate and the suite rate is given on request. See all rooms.

Practical for families

Parking is free for hotel guests and the electricity runs around the clock. The restaurant serves breakfast for $10 per person, and the lobby café is open for the rest of the day.

Out with the children

The Corniche is in front of the hotel for an evening walk, and a short boat ride takes you to Arwad Island. More things to do in Tartus.

Booking

Choose your dates on the booking form to see the price and availability at once, or write to us on WhatsApp at +963 998 730 730. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Book your stay.

Quick answers

How much is a triple room?

$130 per night, tax included. Breakfast is $10 per person.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is free for hotel guests.

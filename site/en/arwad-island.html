Arwad Island: how to visit from Tartus

Syria's only inhabited island lies about 3 km off Tartus, a short boat ride from the city.

Where is Arwad?

Arwad is a small island in the Mediterranean, about 3 km off the coast of Tartus. It is the only inhabited island in Syria.

How to get there

Boats to Arwad leave from the harbour of Tartus, and the crossing takes about 20 minutes. Times and fares change, so ask reception for the day's boats before you set out.

What to see

There are no cars on the island. Narrow lanes run between stone houses to the citadel in the middle of the island, a fortress dating from the 13th century. Along the shore you can see remains of the old sea wall built of huge stone blocks, the fishing harbour, and the yards where wooden boats are still built by hand.

A little history

In antiquity Arwad was a Phoenician island city with its own kings and fleet, and towns on the coast opposite, Amrit among them, depended on it. In 1302 it was the last Crusader position in the Levant to fall.

Before you go

Allow two to three hours for the island. Wear comfortable shoes and carry some cash.

Staying near Arwad

Royal Inn is on the Sea Corniche of Tartus, on the same seafront the boats leave from. See the sea-view rooms or send a booking request.

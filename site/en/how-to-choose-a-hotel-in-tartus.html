How to choose a hotel in Tartus

There are hotels by the sea, in the city centre and along the coast. Six questions help you pick the right one for your stay.

1. Does the room face the sea?

In Tartus the sea is the reason to come. Ask whether your own room has a sea view and a balcony, not just the lobby or the restaurant. At Royal Inn all 65 rooms and suites have a sea-view balcony.

2. Is there 24-hour electricity?

Ask clearly whether the power runs all day and night, because air conditioning, Wi-Fi and lifts depend on it. Royal Inn has 24-hour electricity. Why it matters.

3. Where exactly is it?

On the Corniche you walk out of the door to the sea, the old town and the boats to Arwad. Outside the centre you may need a car for everything. Royal Inn on the map.

4. Does it have the right room for your group?

Check the maximum number of guests per room and whether extra beds are offered. At Royal Inn: two guests in a Standard Room or Junior Suite, three in a Triple Room, four in the Royal Suite with its connecting room.

5. Are the prices written down?

A good sign is a hotel that publishes its rates with tax included. Royal Inn: $90 single, $100 double, $130 triple per night. Room prices.

6. Can you book directly and get a confirmation?

International booking sites do not list hotels in Syria yet, so book with the hotel and ask for a written confirmation with a booking number. At Royal Inn you get a booking number at once and a confirmation letter once the deposit arrives. How it works.

Royal Inn, Sea Corniche, Tartus: 4 stars, sea-view balconies, 24-hour electricity and reception, free parking, from $90 a night with tax. Check availability.

Quick answers

What should I check before booking a hotel in Tartus?

Sea view from your own room, 24-hour electricity, location, the guest limit per room, published prices and a written confirmation.

Which hotels in Tartus are on the Corniche?

Several hotels stand on or near the Sea Corniche. Royal Inn is directly on it, with most rooms facing the sea.

Royal Inn: a story told by the sea

On the Sea Corniche of Tartus, where the city stops and the Mediterranean begins, stands a hotel built so that every guest wakes up to the same view.

Where the city meets the sea

Tartus has looked out to sea for thousands of years. Phoenician Arwad sits on the water just off the coast, the ruins of Amrit lie a few kilometres to the south, and the Crusader cathedral of Tortosa still stands in the old town.

Royal Inn belongs to that same waterfront. It stands on the Sea Corniche, the promenade where the city comes to walk by the water, and its front door opens onto the sea.

Sixty-five windows on the Mediterranean

The Corniche faces west. That one fact shaped the whole hotel: every one of our 65 rooms and suites has its own balcony turned towards the water, so no guest is given the back of the building.

The sea is the first thing you see in the morning and the last thing you see at night.

A day at Royal Inn

Morning begins with coffee on the balcony while the Corniche wakes up below. At midday the light turns the water bright, and the boats cross towards Arwad. In the late afternoon the sun drops towards the horizon and sets straight into the sea in front of the hotel. After dark the lights come on along the promenade and the city stays out by the water. The Corniche, day and night.

The lights never go out

Electricity at Royal Inn runs 24 hours a day, and reception never closes. Whatever time you arrive, after a long drive or a late flight, the door is open, the air conditioning is on and someone is waiting to welcome you by name.

Rooms made for the way families travel

Couples take the Standard Room or the Junior Suite. Friends and small families take the Triple Room with three single beds. For families of four there is the Royal Suite, on a higher floor, together with its own connecting room: two rooms, one door, one view. See every room.

A table, a coffee and a hall for celebrations

Breakfast and meals are served in the hotel restaurant, and the lobby café is where guests meet for coffee and tea. For the big days there is the hotel's own wedding hall, so families can celebrate and stay under one roof, with the sea outside. The wedding hall.

Coming home to Tartus

Many of the people who stay with us are coming home: Syrians who live abroad, back for the summer to see their families. We made booking work for them from anywhere in the world. Send a request online or on WhatsApp, a relative in Syria sends the deposit, and the confirmation letter arrives on your phone. Booking from abroad.

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 12:00.

How do I pay?

In cash at the hotel or by transfer to a Syrian bank, in US dollars. Rates include tax.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is available for $10 per person.

Do all rooms have a sea view?

Yes. Every room and suite has a balcony and a sea view.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is free for hotel guests.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel without charge up to 48 hours before arrival.

Which languages does the team speak?

Arabic, and basic English.

How booking and paying work at Royal Inn

Booking a hotel in Syria works a little differently: there is no online card payment. Here is exactly how it goes, from the first message to check-out.

Booking directly with the hotel

You book directly with Royal Inn, not through a booking platform. Use the booking form on this site, where you choose your dates and see the price and availability at once, or send your dates and the number of guests on WhatsApp or by phone at +963 998 730 730. The hotel replies and confirms the details with you. Send a booking request.

The deposit: a transfer inside Syria

There is no online card payment. To confirm the booking, a deposit is sent by money transfer inside Syria. If you are abroad, a relative or a friend in Syria can send it for you. Once the deposit arrives, the hotel confirms your booking. More on booking from abroad.

The balance: US dollars cash at the hotel

The rest of the stay is paid at the hotel in US dollars cash. Prices include tax, so there is nothing added at reception for the room. Breakfast is optional, at $10 per person per day. See room prices.

Cancellation, check-in and check-out

Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before your arrival date. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 12:00.

Rooms and number of guests

The hotel does not add extra beds. Instead, larger parties take more rooms. The Standard Room and the Junior Suite take up to 2 guests, the Triple Room up to 3, and the Royal Suite with its connecting room up to 4. See the rooms and suites.

Groups, companies and weddings

For a group, a company stay or a wedding, use the group request page so the hotel can prepare an offer for your dates and numbers. Send a group request.

Quick answers

Can I pay online by card?

No. There is no online card payment. A deposit is sent by money transfer inside Syria, and the balance is paid at the hotel in US dollars cash.

I live abroad. How do I pay the deposit?

A relative or friend in Syria can send the deposit for you by money transfer inside Syria. The hotel confirms the booking once it arrives.

Do prices include tax?

Yes. Room prices include tax. Breakfast is optional at $10 per person per day.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before arrival.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00, and check-out is until 12:00.

Can I add an extra bed?

No, the hotel does not add extra beds. Larger parties book more rooms: up to 2 guests in the Standard Room or Junior Suite, 3 in the Triple Room and 4 in the Royal Suite with its connecting room.

A hotel in Tartus with 24-hour electricity

For many visitors to Syria, the first question about a hotel is simple: is there electricity all day? At Royal Inn the answer is yes.

Power around the clock

Electricity at Royal Inn runs 24 hours a day, every day. It is not limited to certain hours and it does not depend on the street outside.

What it means for your stay

Air conditioning works through the hottest summer afternoon and all night. Your phone charges whenever you need it. Wi-Fi stays on, so you can call family or work from your room. Children sleep in a cool room, and the balcony light is on when you come back from the Corniche late at night.

Reception that never closes

Reception is open 24 hours too. Whatever time you arrive, someone is there to welcome you, with parking free in front of the hotel. How to find the hotel.

Rooms facing the sea

All 65 rooms and suites have a sea-view balcony. Standard Room from $90 for one and $100 for two, Triple Room $130, tax included. See the rooms or book directly.

Quick answers

Does Royal Inn Tartus have electricity 24 hours?

Yes. Electricity at Royal Inn runs 24 hours a day, and reception is open around the clock.

Is there air conditioning at Royal Inn?

Yes, every room has air conditioning, and with 24-hour electricity it works day and night.

Summer in Tartus with the family

Warm sea, long evenings on the Corniche and relatives to visit: a practical guide for families spending the summer in Tartus.

When to come

The sea is warmest from July to September, around 27 to 28°C, and stays warm into October. Summer is also the busiest time on the coast, so book early. Weather and sea by month.

Which room for your family

Two guests: Standard Room or Junior Suite. Three guests: Triple Room with three single beds. Four guests: the Royal Suite with its own connecting room. Extra beds are not offered, so choose the room for the number of guests. Family rooms.

Booking from abroad

Send the request on the website or WhatsApp. A relative in Syria can pay the deposit by transfer inside Syria, and the balance is paid at the hotel in cash, in US dollars. How booking from abroad works.

A summer day

Do the sights in the cooler morning, rest in an air-conditioned room through the heat of the day (electricity runs 24 hours), and spend the evening on the Corniche, where the city comes out after sunset. If there is a family wedding, the hotel has its own hall.

What to pack

Sun cream, hats and light clothes, comfortable shoes for Arwad and the old town, and cash for boats and small shops.

Most rooms at Royal Inn face the sea. Standard Room from $90, Triple Room $130 a night, tax included. Check your dates.

Quick answers

When is the sea warmest in Tartus?

From July to September, around 27 to 28°C.

Can a family of four stay in one room?

The Royal Suite with its connecting room takes up to four guests. Other rooms take two or three.
